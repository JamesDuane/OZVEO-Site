The OZVEO Process

Enough structure to stay sharp. Enough flexibility to build the right thing.

The process prevents two expensive failures: building before the problem is understood and polishing a system nobody knows how to operate.

Five stages

From current state to working system.

01

Audit

Understand the current website, offer, customer path, and operational friction.

02

Strategy

Decide what the site and systems need to accomplish before designing screens.

03

Build

04

Launch

05

Improve

Use real feedback and performance signals to refine what matters.
